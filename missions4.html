COVID-19 MEDICAL SELF-RELIANCE


  • In early 2020, India imported almost all its PPE kits and ventilators.

  • Within months, local manufacturers scaled up from almost 0 to 450,000+ PPE kits per day.

  • Ventilator production jumped from a few hundred over to 50k units.

  • This rapid response not onlt met domestic demand but also positioned India as an exporter of critical supplies>

VACCINE DEVELOPMENT & EXPORT


  • Covaxin (Bharat Biotech, indigenous) and Covishield(Serum Institute, licensed) were produced at massive scale.

  • India administrated over 2 billion doses domesticallyand exported to 98+ countries under Vaccine Maitri.

  • Showcased biotech strength and proved India could innovate & manufacture life-saving tect at scale.

DEFENSE INDIGENISATION


  • The government issued a "Negative Import List" banning import of 400+ weapons/platforms to force local production.

  • Hindustan Aeronautics Limited(HAL) got ₹48,000 crore contract for 83 Tejas Light Combat Aircraft-all made in India.

  • India's defense exports hit a record ₹21,000+ crore in 2023, up from just ₹900 crore in 2014.

  • Big Shift: India is now just reducing imports, but also exporting defence hardware.

ELECTRONICS & MANUFACTURING GROWTH


  • Mobile phone production under Atmanirbhar + PLI(Production Linked Incentive) scheme skyrocketed.

  • India went from importing most phones in 2014 to exporting $11+ billion worth in 2022-23.

  • Apple, Samsung and other giants now assemble in India.

  • Split Air Conditioners, TVs, and other electronics also saw import substitution.

INFRASTRUCTURE & MSME EMPOWERMENT


  • Massive puch in food processing (PMFME scheme), rail coach factories, shipbuilding(Goa Shipyard's FPVs), and maritime skill huds.

  • Over ₹5 lakh crore in collateral-free loans disbursed to MSMEs, protecting jobs and keeping small industries afloat during COVID.

  • This encured small industries-the backbone of India's economy-survived and expanded.
