Main Goals

The Atmanirbhar Bharat mission aims to:

  • Boost local manufacturing and reduce import dependency.
  • Promote startups and entrepreneurship.
  • Enhance skills of Indian youth.
  • Empower every citizen to contribute to the economy.


Other Important Goals:

  • Build more government hospitals for the incresing population.
  • Provide clean water to every house.
  • Make sure that every citizens are happy and can earn important expenses that are necessary.
  • Decrease the pollution in our country by manufacturing electric vechiles.
